# Mağaza kaydı – İngilizce (en-US)

## Uygulama adı (en fazla 30 karakter)

Pawnic

## Kısa açıklama (en fazla 80 karakter)

No dice, just cards! A strategic race board game with friends or against bots.

## Tam açıklama (en fazla 4000 karakter)

Pawnic is a race board game where you try to bring all your pawns home first. But there are no dice: you decide which card from your hand to play and which pawn to move. Trust your moves, not your luck.

CARDS INSTEAD OF DICE
• Draw a card on your turn and play one from your hand.
• Move ahead with forward cards, or spoil your rivals' plans with back cards.
• Turn the game around in one move with special cards such as Swap, Send Back, Shortcut and Joker.
• Land on a rival pawn to send it back to start; watch out for safe squares and speed squares.

2–4 PLAYERS ON ONE PHONE
• Play with friends or family on a single phone and pass it to whoever's turn it is.
• Choose how many pawns each player has.

SOLO AGAINST BOTS
• Four bot levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Very Hard.
• Bots don't cheat; they can't see the cards you can't see.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
• An unfinished game is saved, so you can pick up where you left off.
• Set the game speed to Slow, Normal or Fast.
• "How to Play" explains the rules with a short demo.
• No internet and no account needed.
• Available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Indonesian.
